**Paradox: cut a string that's holding up a weight, and the weight rises.**

In 1991, Joel Cohen and Paul Horowitz published a setup in *Nature*. A weight hangs from two springs connected by a short, tight string. Two looser "safety" strings also run between them. **Some cities give you a red light on purpose, because a road packed with cars moves fewer of them.**

There are two reasons, and the second is the interesting one.
